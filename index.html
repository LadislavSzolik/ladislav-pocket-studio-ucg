I paint. I film
the painting. Brands get the footage.

I'm Ladi — an acrylic painter with a slow-living studio practice, based in Zurich. A few years ago I started pointing a camera at my own process: mixing colour, testing materials, unboxing what just arrived. That habit turned into a small library of UGC content built specifically for art and craft brands, shot the way an actual working painter uses the product — not a script written around it.

I care about getting the material right on camera as much as I care about getting it right on canvas, and I'm straightforward about the commercial side of that: good content for a brand and honest work for my own practice aren't in tension for me.

Services

Two ways to work together — both grounded in an actual studio practice.

Brand collaborations

Authentic UGC built around your product: unboxings, material tests, process videos, and honest first impressions — filmed the way I'd document my own work, with your product genuinely in use.

  • Product features & unboxings
  • Process & tutorial-style videos
  • Written and recorded voiceover on request

Social takeover

For art-related accounts that want a creator's eye on their channel for a day, a launch, or an ongoing series — shooting and showcasing your products in a real studio context rather than a studio set.

  • One-day or short-run account takeovers
  • Product showcases inside real studio content
